# Nagajani Nagakumaran

**Biomedical Engineer | Machine Learning | Biomedical Imaging**

MSc Biomaterials & Tissue Engineering, University College London  
BEng Biomedical Engineering, Queen Mary University of London

I am a biomedical engineer interested in applying computational methods, biomaterials and quantitative imaging to problems in cancer research and regenerative medicine.

My current research focuses on data-driven modelling of green silver nanoparticle synthesis using machine learning and deep learning. I am developing a large, structured dataset from published experimental studies and extending the modelling pipeline toward deep neural networks and advanced tabular learning methods to investigate how synthesis conditions influence nanoparticle properties.
